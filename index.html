Structure of Indian Democracy

Lok Sabha (Lower House)

Members are directly elected by the public. Elections are held every 5 years to choose the Prime Minister.

Rajya Sabha (Upper House)

Members are elected by state legislative assemblies. It's a permanent body with members retiring every 2 years.

State Legislative Assemblies

Vidhan Sabha elections choose the Chief Minister and form the state government.
